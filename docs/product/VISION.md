# Product Vision

This document owns desired outcomes, success conditions, and guiding product
principles. It does not define implementation contracts.

Turn scattered reports about stray dogs in Creel into a trustworthy, privacy-aware
map and business evidence that the Association can act on, without putting account
barriers in front of the public.

## Success

- Public reporting is fast and remains usable when connectivity is unreliable.
- The public can understand recent activity without unnecessary privacy exposure.
- The Association can analyze and share trustworthy, purpose-appropriate evidence.
- Moderation and duplicate decisions remain attributable, reviewable, and reversible.
- Operational and privacy promises are demonstrated before release rather than
  remaining aspirational prose.

## Principles

1. **Evidence without overclaiming.** Automated signals support people; they do not
   manufacture certainty.
2. **Anonymous collection, realistic content risk.** Do not solicit reporter
   identity; warn and moderate incidental PII rather than promise impossibility.
3. **Resilient participation.** Weak connectivity should not prevent someone from
   recording an observation.
4. **Purpose-limited access.** Each actor receives only the information and actions
   needed for its responsibility.
5. **Reversibility.** Moderation and grouping decisions should remain correctable
   while evidence still serves a legitimate purpose.
